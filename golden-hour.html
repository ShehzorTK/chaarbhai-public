What couples ask before they book

Can we trust you with our wedding day?

We have covered more than 1,800 weddings since 2013. Some families have come back for a second wedding, and in some we have photographed three generations. Every review further down is linked to the original on Google, so you can read what couples wrote in their own words.

Have you shot weddings like ours?

Very likely. We shoot South Asian and multi-day weddings, from the mehndi and nikkah to the baraat, anand karaj, ceremony and reception, and we have photographed Hindu, Muslim, Sikh and Christian weddings. We can cover each event.

Will we feel comfortable around you?

Couples tell us they did. In their reviews they say we made them feel at ease and like family. We start with a meeting to get to know you before the day, so you are not meeting us for the first time on your wedding morning. And we aim for real, candid moments, not a day of stiff posing.

Will you know who matters in our family and what moments we can't miss?

That's what the meeting is for. We ask about your wedding, the coverage you need and the photos you are after, and before the day we ask who the important people are, the ones we must not miss.

Will it feel like our wedding, or like a template?

We don't use presets or a standard editing system. Every photo is edited on its own, so it looks like your day. We can do what's trendy, but what we aim to keep are the real moments, shot in a way that will still look right in many years.

Who exactly will be there on the day?

One photographer and one videographer, with lighting and support, for the ten hours. If your event is busy, with many things happening at once, extra photographers are available as an add-on.

How long until we get our photos and film?

Because every photo is edited individually, delivery can take up to three months. If you need it sooner, rushed delivery is available as an add-on.

What does Golden Hour cost, and is there anything extra?

$5,500 CAD for ten hours, with every photo individually edited, a private online gallery, and your wedding film by private download link. Extra hours are $550 each, and add-ons such as extra photographers, drone footage with an additional videographer, and rushed delivery are priced separately. Planning a longer weekend? Ask us about our larger packages.

How do we book, and what if plans change?

Start with the form below. After we meet, we send a contract in writing. Booking takes an initial deposit, and the rest is paid later, as set out in the contract. Because we reserve your date for you alone, a cancellation by the client is not refundable. Please read our Refunds and Cancellation page for the full details.
