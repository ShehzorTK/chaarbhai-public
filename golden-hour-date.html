What it costs

Most couples invest between $3,500 and $5,500 CAD for a full wedding day, depending on photo, film or both. Send us your date and we’ll send the full pricing, with nothing extra you didn’t ask for.

What's included
  • Ten hours with one photographer, one videographer or both, with lighting and support, from getting ready to the last dance.
  • With photo: every photo edited individually, never from a preset, in a private online gallery.
  • With video: your wedding film by private download link, with professional audio and on-camera interviews.
  • A get-ready session, and coverage of each event you choose: nikkah, baraat, anand karaj, mehndi or reception.

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Photo only and video only are the same ten hours with just that half. Extra hours are $550 each, whichever option you choose. A deposit holds your date and the rest is paid later, as set out in your contract. Planning a longer weekend? Ask us about our larger packages.

Frequently asked questions

Can we trust you with our wedding day?

We have covered more than 1,800 weddings since 2008. Some families have come back for a second wedding, and in some we have photographed three generations. Every review further down is linked to the original on Google, so you can read what couples wrote in their own words.

Have you shot weddings like ours?

Very likely. We shoot South Asian and multi-day weddings, from the mehndi and nikkah to the baraat, anand karaj, ceremony and reception, and we have photographed Hindu, Muslim, Sikh and Christian weddings. We can cover each event, and we know what the important moments are before they happen.

Will we feel comfortable around you?

Most of our brides hate being photographed too, and they tell us they felt at ease. In their reviews they say we made them feel at ease and like family. We start with a conversation to get to know you before the day, so you are not meeting us for the first time on your wedding morning. And we aim for real, candid moments, not a day of stiff posing.

Will you know who matters in our family and what moments we can't miss?

That's what our first conversation is for. We ask about your wedding, the coverage you need and the photos you are after, and before the day we ask who the important people are, the ones we must not miss.

Will it feel like our wedding, or like a template?

We don't use presets or a standard editing system. Every photo is edited on its own, so it looks like your day. We can do what's trendy, but what we aim to keep are the real moments, shot in a way that will still look right in many years.

Who exactly will be there on the day?

One photographer and one videographer, with lighting and support, for the ten hours. If your event is busy, with many things happening at once, extra photographers are available as an add-on.

How long until we get our photos and film?

Because every photo is edited individually, delivery can take up to three months. If you need it sooner, rushed delivery is available as an add-on.

What does Golden Hour cost, and is there anything extra?

Ten hours is $5,500 CAD for photo and video, $3,500 CAD for photo only, or $4,500 CAD for video only, with every photo individually edited, a private online gallery, and your wedding film by private download link (photo only has the photos, video only has the film). Extra hours are $550 each, whichever option you choose, and add-ons such as extra photographers, drone footage with an additional videographer, and rushed delivery are priced separately. Planning a longer weekend? Ask us about our larger packages.

How do we book, and what if plans change?

Start by sending us your date in the form above. After the consult, we send a contract in writing. Booking takes an initial deposit, and the rest is paid later, as set out in the contract. Because we reserve your date for you alone, a cancellation by the client is not refundable. Please read our Refunds and Cancellation page for the full details.
