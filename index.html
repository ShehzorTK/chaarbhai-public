The photographs your family keeps

We photograph and film weddings, and have done since 2013. Over eighteen hundred of them so far. We’re based in the Greater Toronto Area and we travel.

We plan the boring parts

We sit down with you before the day and go through it properly. Which events matter most, who has to be in the family photos, what you want us nowhere near.

It’s not the interesting part of the job. It’s the reason things don’t get missed.

We stay out of the way

The aim is to be as unobtrusive as we can, so most of the day gets documented as it actually happens. When a shot needs setting up we’ll step in and direct it, then get out of the way again.
